# Source and Legacy Coverage — v8.0

Course: TGS-2023020567 — Unlocking Business Potential with Strategic Negotiation Tactics  
Release date: 16 August 2026

## Legacy deck coverage retained and rebuilt

The supplied 109-slide v7 deck was used as the coverage floor. The v8 single-source build retains and modernises:

- negotiation purpose, stages and integrative/distributive bargaining;
- outcomes, BATNA, WATNA, walk-away limits and negotiation planning;
- roles, responsibilities, venue, timing, agenda and information exchange;
- negotiation styles, interpersonal capability, social and cultural context;
- opening offers, anchoring, framing and evidence-backed proposals;
- active listening, empathy, emotional regulation and personal attacks;
- pressure, delay, authority, good-cop/bad-cop and moral-pressure tactics;
- mutual-gain options, concessions, impasse, consensus and agreement terms;
- walk-away judgement, closure, implementation records and debriefing.

Stale administration, Jamboard references, irrelevant AI-ethics activity text and unsupported tactical claims were not reproduced. Their instructional intent was replaced with current course-aligned content and real-world negotiation activities.
