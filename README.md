# Unlocking Business Potential with Strategic Negotiation Tactics

WSQ course materials for **TGS-2023020567 — Business Negotiation (ICT-BIN-3105-1)**.

This package develops practical preparation and bargaining capability through ten connected, workplace-realistic activities. The slide deck is visual and concept-led; detailed procedures, scenarios, questions, evidence checkpoints and acceptance criteria are provided in the Learner Guide and each activity folder.

## Learning outcomes

- Identify negotiation outcomes, context and relevant background information required to prepare for negotiation.
- Apply negotiation techniques during negotiation and record negotiations for post-negotiation evaluation.

## Activity portfolio

1. Renew a critical ingredient supply agreement.
2. Create a SaaS renewal team mandate.
3. Prepare a commercial lease renewal.
4. Plan a Singapore–Japan distribution partnership.
5. Design a total-reward job offer negotiation.
6. Anchor and frame an agency retainer.
7. Recover a logistics service relationship.
8. Break a construction variation impasse.
9. Negotiate a multi-party growth investment.
10. Run and debrief a strategic sustainability partnership.

Open [activities/README.md](activities/README.md) to access the individual activity folders. Every activity Markdown resource is also supplied as a same-basename printable PDF.
