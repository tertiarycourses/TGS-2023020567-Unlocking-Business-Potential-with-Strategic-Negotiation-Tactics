# Evidence Checklist — Activity 6

## Anchor and Frame an Agency Retainer

- [ ] Verified facts, working hypotheses and training assumptions are separated.
- [ ] Every scenario question is answered with evidence or a stated limitation.
- [ ] The detailed procedure was followed and each checkpoint is recorded.
- [ ] Interests, authority, owners, conditions and dates are explicit where applicable.
- [ ] Acceptance test met: The opening is ambitious but evidence-backed, covers at least six terms and includes no unconditional concession.
- [ ] The final recommendation states residual risk and the next validation action.
