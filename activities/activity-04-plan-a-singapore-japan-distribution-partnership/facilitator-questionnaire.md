# Facilitator Questionnaire — Activity 4

## Plan a Singapore–Japan Distribution Partnership

1. Which cultural observations are facts, and which are hypotheses to test?
2. What respectful process questions should clarify authority and consensus?
3. How should the agenda balance relationship-building and commercial progress?
4. What safeguards prevent ambiguity across language versions?

## Challenge questions

1. Which claim is a verified fact, which is a hypothesis, and which still needs evidence?
2. What would cause your target, reservation point or package to change?
3. Which stakeholder, authority limit or implementation risk could invalidate the plan?
4. How will you demonstrate an executable agreement rather than a verbal understanding?
