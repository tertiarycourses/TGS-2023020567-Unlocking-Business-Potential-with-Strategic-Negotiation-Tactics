# Facilitator Questionnaire — Activity 10

## Run and Debrief a Strategic Sustainability Partnership

1. What outcomes, BATNAs, roles, evidence and cultural/process assumptions should each team prepare?
2. Which opening package creates a credible anchor across value, risk and implementation?
3. How should teams respond to pressure, impasse and late requests while preserving reciprocity?
4. Does the final record make commitments, owners, evidence and review conditions executable?

## Challenge questions

1. Which claim is a verified fact, which is a hypothesis, and which still needs evidence?
2. What would cause your target, reservation point or package to change?
3. Which stakeholder, authority limit or implementation risk could invalidate the plan?
4. How will you demonstrate an executable agreement rather than a verbal understanding?
