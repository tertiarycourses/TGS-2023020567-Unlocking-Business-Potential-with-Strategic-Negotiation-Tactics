# Facilitator Questionnaire — Activity 3

## Prepare a Commercial Lease Renewal

1. Which comparables are genuinely relevant, and what adjustments are required?
2. What is the total value of rent, incentive, duration and break rights?
3. Which three packages are equivalent to Orchid but differently attractive to the landlord?
4. Which facts and assumptions should be disclosed, protected or verified?

## Challenge questions

1. Which claim is a verified fact, which is a hypothesis, and which still needs evidence?
2. What would cause your target, reservation point or package to change?
3. Which stakeholder, authority limit or implementation risk could invalidate the plan?
4. How will you demonstrate an executable agreement rather than a verbal understanding?
