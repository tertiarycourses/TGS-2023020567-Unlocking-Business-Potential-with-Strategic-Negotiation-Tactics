# Facilitator Questionnaire — Activity 7

## Recover a Logistics Service Relationship

1. Which facts, interpretations and emotions must be separated?
2. What open and diagnostic questions could reveal the failure system?
3. How can the team acknowledge impact without accepting unsupported blame?
4. What reciprocal recovery commitments create measurable improvement?

## Challenge questions

1. Which claim is a verified fact, which is a hypothesis, and which still needs evidence?
2. What would cause your target, reservation point or package to change?
3. Which stakeholder, authority limit or implementation risk could invalidate the plan?
4. How will you demonstrate an executable agreement rather than a verbal understanding?
