# Facilitator Questionnaire — Activity 1

## Renew a Critical Ingredient Supply Agreement

1. What are the organisation's must-have, target, tradeable and relationship outcomes?
2. What is the best credible BATNA, and what evidence is needed to validate it?
3. Which issues could create value beyond unit price?
4. What opening package and reservation point should the sponsor approve?

## Challenge questions

1. Which claim is a verified fact, which is a hypothesis, and which still needs evidence?
2. What would cause your target, reservation point or package to change?
3. Which stakeholder, authority limit or implementation risk could invalidate the plan?
4. How will you demonstrate an executable agreement rather than a verbal understanding?
