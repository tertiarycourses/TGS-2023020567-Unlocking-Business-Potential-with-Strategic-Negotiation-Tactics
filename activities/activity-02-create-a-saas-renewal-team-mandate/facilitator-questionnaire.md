# Facilitator Questionnaire — Activity 2

## Create a SaaS Renewal Team Mandate

1. Who owns the commercial outcome and who can bind the organisation?
2. Which specialists must attend, remain on call or review offline?
3. What commitments can the lead make without escalation?
4. How should the team handle internal disagreement during the vendor meeting?

## Challenge questions

1. Which claim is a verified fact, which is a hypothesis, and which still needs evidence?
2. What would cause your target, reservation point or package to change?
3. Which stakeholder, authority limit or implementation risk could invalidate the plan?
4. How will you demonstrate an executable agreement rather than a verbal understanding?
