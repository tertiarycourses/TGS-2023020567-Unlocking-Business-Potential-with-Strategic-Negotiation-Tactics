# Facilitator Questionnaire — Activity 5

## Design a Total-Reward Job Offer Negotiation

1. What are the employer's target, reservation point and non-financial priorities?
2. Which candidate interests should be tested rather than assumed?
3. How can three packages create choice while protecting internal equity?
4. Which candidate information is relevant, confidential or inappropriate to request?

## Challenge questions

1. Which claim is a verified fact, which is a hypothesis, and which still needs evidence?
2. What would cause your target, reservation point or package to change?
3. Which stakeholder, authority limit or implementation risk could invalidate the plan?
4. How will you demonstrate an executable agreement rather than a verbal understanding?
