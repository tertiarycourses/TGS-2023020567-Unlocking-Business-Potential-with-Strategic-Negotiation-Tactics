# Facilitator Questionnaire — Activity 9

## Negotiate a Multi-Party Growth Investment

1. Which parties can form supportive or blocking coalitions?
2. Which terms are economically linked even if negotiated separately?
3. What precedent comparisons are misleading without context?
4. Which package best balances capital, control, incentives and relationship value?

## Challenge questions

1. Which claim is a verified fact, which is a hypothesis, and which still needs evidence?
2. What would cause your target, reservation point or package to change?
3. Which stakeholder, authority limit or implementation risk could invalidate the plan?
4. How will you demonstrate an executable agreement rather than a verbal understanding?
